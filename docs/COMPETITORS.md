# Competitive Landscape (checked October 21, 2026)

*Who else ranks, summarizes or digests what people follow, at what price, and
how Weirling differs. Every price below has a source; the public comparison
pages (`/vs/...`) use the same numbers and show the same date (#168).*

| Product | Price (Oct 2026) | AI and personalization | vs Weirling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feedly** | Free (100 sources); Pro about $6 to $7/mo billed yearly; Pro+ about $12/mo billed yearly, first-year offer $99 [1][2] | AI feeds and priorities trained on topics, keywords and examples (Pro+) | Rules you maintain vs ranking learned from reading; explained per item |
| **Inoreader** | Free (150 feeds); Pro $7.50/mo billed yearly or $9.99 monthly, 2,500 feeds [3] | Intelligence: summaries, mentions, tags, reports; bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Mistral key | Has AI, but no ranking by reading behavior |
| **NewsBlur** | Free (64 sites); Premium $36/yr; Archive $99/yr; Pro $29/mo [4] | Trainable per-user classifier (likes and dislikes of keywords, authors, tags); open source, self-hostable | **Closest analog.** Reading telemetry and meaning vs keyword training |
| **Folo** | Free (150 feeds); Basic $49.99/yr; Plus $99.99/yr; Pro $999.99/yr [5] | AI summaries and translation, rule-based actions, bring your own key on Plus and Pro; AGPL-3.0 [6] | A fast AI timeline of everything vs one digest ranked for you |
| **Readwise Reader** | $9.99/mo billed yearly or $12.99 monthly; 30-day trial [7] | Read-later with highlights and Ghostreader prompts | Read-later focus; no ranking by reading behavior |
| **Readless** | Pro $4.90/mo; Max $9/mo; 7-day trial with card; no free or annual plan [8] | AI newsletter and RSS digests, topics covered by several senders, synthesis briefing on Max | Summaries of everything vs a ranked, explained digest |
| **Meco** | Free; PRO $3.99/mo or $34.99/yr [9] | Newsletter inbox with AI summaries | Newsletters only |

**Mailbrew** became free under new ownership in November 2025 and is not
actively developed [9]; leave it out of active comparisons. **Particle**,
**Brief Digest**, **Daigest** and **auto-news** were not rechecked this round.

## What is uniquely true

No one combines **ranking learned from reading telemetry, per-user learned
weights, an explanation for every pick, and full self-hosting**. Each piece has
a competitor (NewsBlur trains rankings, Inoreader and Folo add AI, Readless
sends digests); the combination is still open. Market that provable version,
not "no one does this."

## Price positioning

Hosted plans (decision D-18): **Reader $6/mo or $54/yr, Pro $12/mo or
$108/yr**, a 30-day Pro trial, no free hosted plan. Self-hosting is free with
every feature (MIT).

- Reader at $6 sits with Feedly Pro and under Inoreader Pro ($7.50) and
  Readwise ($9.99), above Readless Pro ($4.90).
- Pro at $12 matches Feedly Pro+ and adds what none of them sell: ranking by
  reading behavior, explained per item, plus API and MCP access.
- Don't race the $3 to $5 summary apps on price; justify the price on
  capability, and point price-sensitive power users to self-hosting.

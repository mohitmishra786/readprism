## Why Weirling

- **Ranked by you.** It learns from what you open, finish, skip and rate,
  not from what is popular.
- **Explains itself.** Every item says why it is there, using the same
  numbers that ranked it.
- **Private by default.** Self-hosted, no third-party trackers, and the only
  outbound calls are the ones you configure. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).
- **Yours to keep.** MIT licensed, with a full data export at any time.

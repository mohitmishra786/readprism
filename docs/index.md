# ReadPrism

Personalized content intelligence, self-hosted. ReadPrism aggregates your
feeds, newsletters and creators into one ranked daily digest — ranked by how
*you actually read*, with an explanation for every item.

- **Behavioral ranking**: eight pre-consumption signals, per-user learned
  weights, honest explanations (exact w·f contributions).
- **Self-hosted & private**: everything local; the only outbound calls are
  the ones you configure. No third-party telemetry.
- **MIT licensed** (D-13).

# Embedding models on real articles (#203)

52 openly licensed articles from `backend/tests/fixtures/real_pages` (13 each from the Go blog, Rust blog, Kubernetes blog and Wikipedia; licenses per article in `manifest.json`). Generated by `backend/scripts/embedding_eval.py` on 2026-10-21, CPU only.

| Model | Dim | Max tokens | Title to article MRR (R@1) | Summary to article MRR (R@1) | Same-source neighbours P@5 | Docs/s | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` | 384 | 256 | 0.928 (0.87) | 0.980 (0.96, n=50) | 0.800 | 68.8 | current default |
| `bge-small-en-v1.5` | 384 | 512 | 0.952 (0.90) | 0.990 (0.98, n=50) | 0.788 | 26.3 | same 384-d column |
| `nomic-embed-text-v1.5` | 768 | 8192 | 0.710 (0.63) | 0.786 (0.76, n=50) | 0.750 | 0.6 | 768-d, 8k context |
| `bge-m3` | 1024 | 8192 | 0.981 (0.96) | 0.990 (0.98, n=50) | 0.788 | 0.2 | 1024-d, 8k context, multilingual |

- **Title to article:** the page title is the query; all article bodies are the corpus.
- **Summary to article:** an LLM-written brief of the article is the query (no shared title).
- **Neighbours P@5:** share of each article's 5 nearest articles from the same source.
- **Docs/s:** encoding speed of full article bodies on this machine's CPU.

## What it shows

- **bge-small-en-v1.5 is the better default.** It finds the right article
  more often than MiniLM (title MRR 0.952 vs 0.928; summary 0.990 vs
  0.980) with the same 384-d vectors, so it fits the existing
  `content_items.embedding` column. Switching means re-embedding, not a
  migration. It is 2.6 times slower to encode, which is still fast (26
  articles a second on a laptop CPU).
- **bge-m3 is the most accurate** (title MRR 0.981) and multilingual, but at
  0.2 articles a second on CPU (about 5 s per full article) it is too slow
  to embed every ingested item on a hosted CPU box. Worth revisiting only
  with a GPU or for non-English readers.
- **Nomic v1.5 did worst here** (title MRR 0.710). The score is identical
  under transformers 4 and 5 (its remote code needs a small shim on 5, see
  the script), so this is the model on this data, not a loading bug. The
  planned 768-d Nomic cut-over (#244) is not supported by this evidence.
- **Same-source neighbours** were 0.75 to 0.80 for every model: grouping by
  source doesn't separate them, so it says little about interest matching.

## Limits

52 English technical articles from four sources is small, and titles share
words with their articles, which favours every model. The summaries are a
harder, independent paraphrase test. Full articles were encoded whole; long
context models might do better on chunked text. Re-run on reader data before
any switch, ideally with real queries from search logs.

## Reproduce

From `backend/`:

```bash
python scripts/embedding_eval.py --out ../docs/eval/embedding-models.md
```

The summaries come from `docs/eval/summary-models.jsonl` (#225). Model
weights download on first run (about 3 GB for all four).
